About Me

Hello! My name is Erasyl Nuraddinov. I come from Kazaly District in the Kyzylorda Region of Kazakhstan. I study IT Management at Kazakh-British Technical University (KBTU) and work as a mathematics curator at JUZ40 Online.

My interests bring together technology, mathematics, and education. I enjoy solving problems, learning new skills, and helping students become more confident in mathematics.

Work Experience

Technical Skills

  • Python: Writing programs and solving computational problems.
